### Scenario

After just finishing his exam, Ben is rushing from COE to the Langkit Shop to buy merch. Upon arriving, he notices a very long line made up mostly of first years trying to purchase PE uniforms. He is willing to wait for a while because he really wants to buy, but he still needs to browse through the inventory to see what he likes. As he looks around the shop, more and more people keep queueing, which makes the line even longer than when he arrived. Ben checks the time and notices that he is only a few minutes away from his next class, and he calculates that even if he lines up now after selecting an item, he will still be late. Initially, he hopes that the line will be shorter after he finishes choosing an item, but he ends up having to go to class without being able to purchase anything.

### What this means for my interface

1. Because Ben wants to browse through the store while still being part of the queue, my interface must include a dual queue system that queues those that already paying and those that are still browsing.
2. Because there is a need for a dual queue system, my interface must lobby the browsing queue and prioritize the paying queue if the concerned customer has not finished browsing yet regardless of who queued first.
3. Because Ben has poor eyesight, my interface must go for a more minimalist approach with large font sizes to enhance visual clairty and legibility.

### Scenario

Lola chooses to render her duty at the Langkit shop in the morning because she assumes there are fewer people during that period. However, it just so happens that during her duty, the freshmen who want to buy PE uniforms gather, which makes it really hard for her to keep track of everything going on given her shy nature. Because she is alone on duty, she is left to deal with all the customers alone, which makes her productivity a lot lower and further lengthens the queueing time of each customer.

### What this means for my interface
